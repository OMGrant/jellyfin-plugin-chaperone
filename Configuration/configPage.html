Chaperone

Fills in missing parental ratings for music (Deezer), movies & shows (TMDb), and anime (MyAnimeList) so parental controls work across the whole library.

Library scan

Run a one-off pass over your whole library, filling in ratings for items that don't have one yet (or all items when "Overwrite existing ratings" is on). Your current settings are saved first. Progress appears under Dashboard → Scheduled Tasks.

Settings

Content types

Give each album the least-restrictive rating among its tracks, so parental controls don't hide the album container for lacking a rating of its own.
When Deezer and MusicBrainz can't identify a track, give it its album's rating before falling back to the "Unidentifiable music rating" above.

Stamping every artist TV-G keeps the folder open; the real filtering still happens on the albums and tracks inside. Leave on unless you rate artists yourself.
